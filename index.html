01 / THE MODEL

Start with three truths

There is no single algorithm.

Feed, Stories, Explore, Reels, Search and other surfaces rank for different viewing behaviors.

Sends unlock discovery.

Likes matter more for follower reach; sends matter more when Instagram evaluates non-follower reach.

Every Reel auditions.

A Reel is tested with a likely-to-enjoy audience and can expand with response—not because of follower count alone.

Working rule: make the topic legible to a stranger, win attention, hold it, then earn a meaningful action. Eligibility gets a post into consideration; it never guarantees distribution.
● VERIFIED LIVE

The canonical model

Instagram says each part of the app uses its own algorithm, tailored to how people use that surface. Reels distribution uses watch time, retention, shares, likes and comments, plus audience matching.

How to apply it: Design each post for the surface it will live on — Feed, Stories, Explore, Reels and Search each rank for different viewing behavior.

Mosseri identifies watch time, likes, and sends as the top ranking signals. Likes are slightly more important for connected reach; sends are slightly more important for unconnected reach.

02 / RANKING

What the system predicts

● VERIFIED LIVE

Recommendations expand in stages

A Reel is first shown to a small audience likely to enjoy it. Strong performers move to progressively larger groups. Instagram says this distributes on engagement, not only follower count.

How to apply it: Give every Reel a fair audition: make it legible to a small, likely-to-enjoy audience and let their response — not your follower count — decide how far it travels.

Read rates, not raw reach

Use engagement rates in Insights to explain performance, separating connected reach from followers and unconnected reach from non-followers. Likes, comments, saves, sends, and watch time matter in different contexts.

How to apply it: Compare posts by their like, comment, save and send rates, split by followers vs. non-followers, instead of judging by raw view counts.

Feed AI scores distinct outcomes

01 Enjoy

Likes, comments, reshares, time spent and media type help predict whether someone will enjoy a post.

02 Send

Informative content—news, product reviews, tutorials and how-tos—plus recency, time spent and share-sheet opens help predict DM sharing.

03 Continue

Carousel completion, profile visits, sound-on behavior and whether the first post keeps someone in Feed are separate predictions.

How to apply it: Build posts that can win on more than one prediction at once — enjoyment, a DM send, and keeping the viewer in Feed.

Reach myths Instagram has rejected

Saving as a draft does not affect distribution; interacting with similar content is not required; saying “link in bio” does not reduce reach; toggling a public account private and public does not boost reach; and deleting then reposting a flop is not a recovery tactic. Ranking receives small adjustments every day rather than rare, giant resets.

How to apply it: Stop spending effort on draft-saving rituals, interaction warm-ups, private/public toggling and delete-and-repost rescues. Instagram has rejected every one.

What Instagram actually says

“Post regularly” and “post consistently.” The logic is opportunity: every Reel can reach a new audience, so repeated publishing creates repeated chances to learn and be discovered.

How to apply it: Pick a cadence you can repeat. “Post regularly” and “post consistently” are the whole instruction — there is no quota and no best hour.

10 / ORIGINALITY

Originality is a distribution rule

Counts as original

  • Photos or videos you took
  • A designed photo series, how-to or visual story
  • Material edits that add information or a distinct perspective
  • On-content text or graphics that provide new information
  • A Remix that meaningfully transforms the source

Does not count

  • Re-uploading someone else’s work
  • Adding only a border or watermark
  • Adding only subtitles
  • Credit in the caption without creative input
  • Minor edits that merely restate the source
● VERIFIED LIVE

Current recommendation penalty

As of Apr 30, 2026, accounts that primarily post unoriginal Reels, photos or carousels are removed from recommendation surfaces. Followers can still see the content. Instagram said 75% of US recommendations were then coming from original posts.

How to apply it: Audit your recent posts: if most of what you post is not original, you are out of recommendation surfaces until the rolling 30 days turn over.

● VERIFIED LIVE

Recovery path

An account can become eligible again when most of its recent posts across a rolling 30-day period are original. Check and appeal through Account Status.

How to apply it: Rebuild eligibility with original posts until most of your last 30 days are original, then check and appeal through Account Status.

Eligibility scope ↗
● VERIFIED LIVE

Content and account patterns excluded from recommendations

Safety

Self-harm, suicide or eating-disorder content; violence; sexually explicit or suggestive content; and regulated products including tobacco, vaping, alcohol, firearms, drugs, weight-loss products/services and cosmetic procedures.

Publishing quality

Unoriginal content with only minor edits; long captions unrelated to the underlying content; and coordinated comment networks intended to inflate distribution.

Integrity

False or misleading content, purchased likes or misleading follower-building, repeat fact-check failures, and accounts barred from advertising.

Profiles

Non-recommendable material in a name, username or bio; regular age-inappropriate content; and repeated recent content prominently featuring a photorealistic AI-generated persona.

How to apply it: Screen every post against the four exclusion groups — safety, publishing quality, integrity and profile content — before it goes out.

Recommendation checklist is restated. Use original content, remove watermarks, add audio, keep videos to three minutes or less, and keep the account in good standing.

Hashtags are capped at five. @creators sets a maximum of five hashtags per post or Reel.

APR 2026

Originality expands. Photos and carousels join Reels under the unoriginal-content recommendation policy; “Your Algorithm” expands to Explore.

SEP 2025

The many-Stories reach bug is fixed. Earlier advice to hold back after posting many Stories no longer applies.

MAR–JUN 2026

Workflow myths are closed. Scheduling does not hurt reach; posts rank independently; there is no universal best posting time; grid reordering becomes available.

External-editing and ranking language are clarified. Other editors are fine without their wordmarks; Mosseri emphasizes originality, recency, breaking content, and the followed/not-followed split.

Common claims checked against the record

NOT SUPPORTED

“Post exactly X times per week” or “at the universal best time.” Instagram says regular and consistent, but gives no canonical quota or universal posting time here.

OUTDATED

“Use 30 hashtags.” The current cap is five; Instagram says hashtags aid search rather than directly driving reach.

FALSE

“Follower count controls distribution.” Reels are explicitly tested and expanded based on audience response.

INCOMPLETE

“Watch percentage is all that matters.” Instagram also evaluates total seconds watched, retention, sends, likes and comments.

NOT FOUND

“Keywords in captions are the official growth hack.” Topic matching is official; keyword stuffing was not found in Instagram’s recent guidance.

NOT FOUND

“Carousels always beat single photos.” Carousel completion is a real prediction, but no universal format-winner rule was found.

FALSE

“Scheduling, drafts, external editing, or saying ‘link in bio’ hurts reach.” Instagram has explicitly rejected each claim; only other-app watermarks/wordmarks are the stated external-editor issue.

FALSE

“Wait for a post to peak before publishing again.” Posts rank independently. Avoid posting only within a few hours of the last post.

DISALLOWED

“Pods and purchased engagement help distribution.” Coordinated comment networks and purchasing likes are recommendation risks, not durable growth tactics.

FALSE

“AI content is throttled just for being AI.” Mosseri, Oct 30, 2026: Instagram does not throttle AI content for being AI content. The separate, real consequence is at profile level — an unlabeled profile featuring an AI-generated person can lose reach and recommendation eligibility.
